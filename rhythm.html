🎵 FOR NOTES THAT ACTUALLY MATCH THE MUSIC
Click AUDIO FILE (or drag a track onto the window). The waveform is analysed directly — onset detection, tempo estimate, notes placed on real drum hits and assigned to lanes by frequency, so bass lands left and hats land right.
YouTube mode can't do this: the iframe gives the page no audio, so those charts are an estimate on a steady grid.

Notes feel off? Open Options → Audio Offset, or run the auto-calibrator mid-song.
